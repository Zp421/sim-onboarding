# sim-onboarding




For part 1, I use equations of torque, force on the wheel, and acceleration to simulate the driver pressing the pedal for some time, then holding it



For part 2, I use equations for slip angle, lateral force, lateral acceleration, and lateral velocity to simulate the driver turning the steering wheel to a certain angle, then holding it.



For part3, I use equations of Drag, net acceleration, and velocity to simulate the driver stopping, accelerating, and waiting for the drag to slow the car down.



For part4, I use equations of Propulsion force, force, velocity,accleration, torque, force on wheel, to simulates motor model and how it works after the driver holds it at full throttle for some time



For part 5, I use equations of braking force, acceleration,  and velocity to simulate the driver pressing the brake pedal and how long it takes to stop.
